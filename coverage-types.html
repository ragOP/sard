Coverage types explained

Every Coverage Type —
At The Lowest Available Rate

Our agents find the right coverage for your situation and make sure you're not paying a dollar more than you should.

🛡️
Liability Only
Meets your state's legal minimum. Covers damage you cause to others. Best for older vehicles where full coverage costs more than the car is worth.
From $19/month
🚗
Full Coverage
Liability + collision + comprehensive. Covers your vehicle and others in nearly any scenario. Required by most lenders — but most drivers pay far too much for it.
Lowest rate in 2 minutes
💥
Collision Coverage
Covers your vehicle when you're in an at-fault accident. Your car gets repaired or replaced regardless of who caused the crash. Often bundled with comprehensive.
Compare collision rates
🌩️
Comprehensive
Covers non-collision events — theft, weather, fire, vandalism, hitting an animal. Often cheaper than drivers expect and critical in high-theft or weather-prone areas.
Often under $15/month extra
📄
Gap Insurance
If your car is totaled and you owe more than it's worth, gap covers the difference. Essential for new cars or those with large loan balances. Dealers charge 3x what agents do.
Don't buy it at the dealership
📋
State Minimum
Every state sets a minimum coverage requirement. Our agents know exactly what your state requires and find the cheapest policy that keeps you legally covered and nothing more.
Meet minimum, save maximum
